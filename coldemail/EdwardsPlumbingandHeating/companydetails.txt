FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

Edwards Plumbing & Heating (legally incorporated as Edwards Plumbing & Heating Contractors, Inc.) is one of the oldest, largest, and most respected plumbing and HVAC contracting companies in Montgomery and the greater River Region of Alabama.

Founded in 1948 by master craftsman Charles W. Edwards, the company has operated continuously for over 75 years and now spans four generations of family stewardship. Today, leadership is spearheaded by President Chuck Edwards (Charles W. Edwards, Jr.), Vice President Charlie Edwards, and HR/Front Office Operations Manager Sally Edwards Meacham.

The company operates from its centralized corporate facility located at 1540 Jean Street in Montgomery, AL (P.O. Box 70399). With a workforce of approximately 65 team members and a fleet of 45 fully equipped service and installation vehicles, Edwards Plumbing & Heating handles high-volume residential service calls, light commercial projects, and large-scale new construction installations across Montgomery, Autauga, and Elmore counties (including Prattville, Millbrook, and Wetumpka).

Comprehensive services provided include:
1. Plumbing Division: 24/7 emergency leak repairs, residential and commercial plumbing installations, tank and tankless water heater installation and replacement, sewer and water main line replacements, hydro-jetting, drain cleaning, repiping, and bathroom/kitchen fixture upgrades.
2. HVAC Division: Complete heating and cooling system installations, energy-efficient heat pumps, gas furnaces, air conditioning maintenance, seasonal tune-ups, system diagnostics, and ductwork fabrication.
3. New Construction & Commercial Contracting: Full plumbing and mechanical contracting for homebuilders, commercial developers, and property managers.

The company maintains regular office dispatch hours from Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM, with round-the-clock emergency support routed through a dedicated emergency dispatch line at (334) 213-1519. In addition, the firm is an active member in good standing with the Montgomery Regional Chamber of Commerce for over 38 years and holds top ratings with local River Region homeowners.
