FULL PLAIN-TEXT RESEARCH
==================================================

Solar Technology Alabama (operating the Alabama Solar Technology Center) is a specialized renewable energy engineering and solar contracting firm based in Eva, Alabama, serving Arab, Guntersville, Huntsville, Cullman, Birmingham, and communities across North and Central Alabama.

The business was founded in 2008 by Ron Holland, an electrical engineer who earned his engineering degree from Mississippi State University and spent 20 years in power distribution engineering with the Southern Company (the parent company of Alabama Power). Leveraging this deep utility background, Holland recognized early the vulnerabilities of centralized power grids and the immense value of true energy independence. Since 2011, Holland has lived 100% off the grid at his research and demonstration facility in Eva.

Unlike standard solar sales companies that focus primarily on grid-tied leasing programs, Solar Technology Alabama focuses on true energy self-sufficiency and resilience:
1. Off-Grid & Stand-Alone Solar Power Systems: Custom electrical engineering, load calculations, solar PV array sizing, and professional inverter/charge controller configuration for off-grid homes, farms, workshops, and remote cabins.
2. Battery Storage & Emergency Backup: Integration of high-capacity lithium and deep-cycle battery banks paired with auto-start backup generator systems to provide uninterrupted power during severe weather and grid disruptions.
3. Solar Water Pumping & Agricultural Systems: Turnkey solar pump solutions for deep wells, agricultural irrigation, and livestock watering (certified Bison Pumps partner).
4. Solar Water Heating & Thermal Energy: Direct thermal solar heating systems for residential water supplies.
5. Technical System Design & Engineering Consultation: Professional paid site evaluations, electrical load profiling, and engineering schematics.
