Sunup Solar LLC is a premier local solar energy and electrical contracting company serving New Hampshire, Maine, and Vermont. Established in 2021 and owned by Hunter Judd, the business is based in Auburn, NH (with mailing address in Candia, NH). Sunup Solar is fully licensed and insured and has been accredited by the Better Business Bureau (BBB) since April 2022, holding an A+ rating.

The company offers a comprehensive range of clean energy and home improvement services. These include custom rooftop and ground-mounted solar panel installations, battery backup systems (using Enphase storage products), solar generators, heat pump installations, and general solar repair, cleaning, and maintenance services. To offer complete turn-key installations, they also provide roof replacements and coordinate tree removal services. Unlike many larger national solar companies, Sunup Solar employs 100% in-house installers and does not outsource or subcontract their work, which is a major point of pride and a key selling point for local customers.

Sunup Solar has built a stellar local reputation. On Google Maps, they maintain a 4.9-star rating with 48 reviews. They also hold 5-star ratings on platforms like EnergySage and EcoWatch. Customer testimonials highlight their honest pricing, lack of high-pressure sales tactics, responsive communication, and high-quality craftsmanship. They are frequently recommended in local New Hampshire Facebook groups and community forums as the top local alternative to large-scale national solar corporations.
