McMullin Plumbing is a highly rated residential and commercial plumbing service provider based in Lakeland, Tennessee, serving Memphis, Bartlett, Germantown, Collierville, and the broader Shelby County region. Owned and operated by master plumber Bill McMullin ("Mr. Bill"), the company has earned a reputation for reliability, transparent pricing, and fast response times.

In addition to traditional plumbing repairs—such as leak detection, drain cleaning, pipe repairs, and fixture installations—McMullin Plumbing operates a specialized water heater service under the brand "Mr. Bill's Water Heater Deals" (mrbillswhdeals.com). This arm of the business focuses on high-efficiency tank and tankless water heater installations, replacements, and maintenance deals.
